# Common teacher questions

## How do I mark attendance?

**Faculty → Attendance** (`/faculty/attendance`), for a class you are assigned
in the timetable. Attendance is per student, per timetabled session, per date.

Marking the same session twice on the same date is rejected rather than
duplicated, so a double submission cannot inflate anyone's record.

## I marked attendance wrong. How do I fix it?

Through the same attendance screen. You own the record — the administration
office cannot correct it for you, which is why students are told to come to you
first.

## How do I enter marks?

**Faculty → Marks** (`/faculty/marks`). Pick the section and the assessment,
then enter each mark as a number out of the exam's total. A mark cannot be
negative and the exam total must be greater than zero.

## Why can my students not see the marks I entered?

Because entering is not publishing. A mark moves through three states:

| Status | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Draft | You only |
| Verified | Teachers and admins |
| Published | Everyone, including the student and their parent |

Verification and publication are separate steps on purpose, so a second pair of
eyes can check before anything reaches a student. Until you publish, the
student sees nothing.

## Can I change a mark after publishing it?

Yes, but it is an administrative correction rather than routine editing, and it
should be raised with the examination section. The change is recorded in the
audit log, and the student's result, GPA and your class average all recalculate
from the corrected figure.

## Which classes and students can I see?

Only the subject-and-section combinations assigned to you in the timetable, and
the students enrolled in them. You cannot see a student's attendance or marks in
a subject taught by someone else, and neither can the assistant on your behalf.

If no classes are assigned to you yet, the portal and the assistant will both
say so rather than showing an empty list that looks like missing data.

## How do I see how my class is performing?

**Faculty → Reports** (`/faculty/reports`), and the class summary on
**Faculty → Classes** (`/faculty/classes`). You get the number of students
assessed, the average percentage and the pass rate.

These figures come from **published marks only**. Unreleased marks never appear
in a class average, so a class you have marked but not published will read as
unassessed.

## How do I find students below an attendance threshold?

**Faculty → Reports**, or ask the assistant for the classes you teach. You can
name the threshold; AIMS stores measurements rather than enforcing one fixed
percentage.

## How do I post an announcement to my class?

**Faculty → Announcements** (`/faculty/announcements`). It reaches the classes
you teach. See *Notifications and Announcements*.

## Where do I see my own teaching timetable?

**Faculty → Timetable** (`/faculty/timetable`).

## How do I set up an assignment?

**Faculty → Assignments** (`/faculty/assignments`), where you create the
assignment and review submissions. An assignment is also one of the six
assessment types, so its marks are entered through **Faculty → Marks** like any
other exam.

## Can the assistant enter marks or mark attendance for me?

No. It is read-only across every table. It can tell you what is recorded, which
classes are yours, and where a screen is, but every write goes through the
portal under your own account.

## How do I change my password?

**Profile → Change Password** in the faculty portal. Your current password is
required as well as the new one.
