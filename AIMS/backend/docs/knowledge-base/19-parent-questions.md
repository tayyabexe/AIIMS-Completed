# Common parent questions

## How do I see my child's attendance?

**Attendance** (`/parent/attendance`). It lists each subject with the sessions
held, the sessions attended, and two percentages.

## Why are there two attendance percentages?

The **standard** figure counts Late as attended. The **strict** figure counts
only Present. If your child has been marked Late, the two will differ, and the
gap tells you how often.

Holidays are excluded from both, so they neither help nor hurt the percentage.

## My child's attendance looks wrong. Who do I contact?

The subject teacher for that class. They marked it and they are the only person
who can correct it — the administration office cannot do it for them.

## Why can I not see my child's marks?

Because they have not been published. Marks are Draft, then Verified, then
Published, and both you and your child see published marks only.

An unpublished mark is not a missing mark. Ask the subject teacher whether the
exam has been marked yet, or whether it is marked but not released.

## Can I see marks before my child does?

No. Parents and students see exactly the same published results, at the same
time.

## How do I check the fee balance?

**Fees** (`/parent/fees`), which shows the total payable, what has been paid,
the balance remaining and the due date for each voucher.

## I paid the fee but it still shows as outstanding

If the accounts office has not verified the payment yet, it shows as **pending**
rather than as paid. That is the expected behaviour, not an error, and it clears
on verification.

If it still has not cleared after a reasonable time, contact the accounts office
with your receipt.

## Where is my child's timetable?

**Timetable** (`/parent/timetable`), for the current week.

## How do I request a re-check of my child's paper?

Through the examination section. It is not a portal feature and neither you nor
your child can submit it from AIMS. See *Corrections, Appeals and Re-checks*
for what to bring.

## Can I see all my children in one account?

Yes, if the administration office has linked them to your account. The portal
shows each child and you select between them. If a child is missing, the link
has not been made — contact the administration office.

## Can I use my child's login instead?

You should not. The parent account exists precisely so that you do not have to,
and the institute needs to be able to tell a parent's activity from a student's.
Your own password is changed from **Profile → Change Password**.

## Can the assistant tell me my child's marks or attendance?

No. The chat assistant has no access to student records at all. It explains how
AIMS works and points you at the right screen; the figures themselves are in the
portal.

## Can I message my child's teacher through AIMS?

No. AIMS has notifications and announcements, which are sent by staff, but there
is no parent-to-teacher messaging. Contact the teacher through the institute's
usual channel.

## Who do I contact about what?

Attendance to the subject teacher. Marks and results to the subject teacher,
then the examination section. Re-checks to the examination section. Fees to the
accounts office. The parent-child link, or your own details, to the
administration office. Sign-in problems to IT support.
