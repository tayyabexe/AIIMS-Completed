# The Student portal

## Screens

| Screen | Route | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | `/student` | Attendance, upcoming classes and notices |
| My Courses | `/student/courses` | Enrolled subjects and per-course detail |
| Attendance | `/student/attendance` | Attendance per subject, with percentages |
| Timetable | `/student/timetable` | Your weekly class schedule |
| Results | `/student/results` | Published results, GPA and CGPA |
| Fees | `/student/fees` | Vouchers, balance and payment history |
| Documents | `/student/documents` | Your uploaded documents |
| Notifications | `/student/notifications` | Fee, result and attendance alerts |
| Profile | `/student/profile` | Personal details and password change |

## What you can see

Your own records, and only your own. Everything in the portal is filtered to
your account automatically — there is no way to reach another student's
attendance, marks, results or fees, and the assistant is subject to the same
limit.

## Reading your attendance

Each subject shows total sessions, how many you attended, and two percentages.
The **standard** figure counts Late as attended; the **strict** figure counts
only Present. If they differ, you have been marked Late — see *Attendance*.

## Reading your results

Only **published** marks and results appear. If you have sat an exam and see
nothing, the marks have not been released yet. That is different from having no
marks, and the assistant will tell you which it is.

## Your fees

Vouchers show total payable, paid, and the remaining balance, with a due date.
If you have submitted a payment that has not yet been verified by the accounts
office, it is shown as pending rather than counted as unpaid.

## Your documents

You can view and upload your own identity and academic documents. You cannot
see anyone else's.

## Using the AI assistant

Ask things like "what is my attendance", "when is my next exam", "how much do I
owe", "what is my CGPA", "where do I see my results".

It answers from your own live records, so figures match your portal. It cannot
change anything — it is read-only — and it cannot discuss another student, no
matter how the question is phrased.

For policy questions ("how do I apply for a transcript") it searches official
documentation, and it will tell you if something is not documented rather than
guessing.

## Getting help

The assistant can tell you what is recorded and where to find it. It cannot
correct a record. For corrections: attendance to your subject teacher, marks to
the examination section, fees to the accounts office, and personal details to
the administration office.
