# Admissions and enrolment

## Becoming a student

Admission is performed by an administrator through the Admin portal
(**Students → Admit**). It creates three things at once: a `users` account, a
`students` record, and the login credentials.

Every admitted student is assigned:

- a **registration number**, unique and permanent, in the form `YYYY-AIMS-REG-NNNN`
- a **programme** (for example BS Computer Science)
- a **batch**, which is the intake year group
- a **section** within that batch
- a **current semester**

## Academic status

A student record carries one of six statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending Verification | Admitted, documents not yet verified |
| Active | Studying normally |
| Suspended | Temporarily barred from classes |
| Withdrawn | Left before completing |
| Graduated | Completed the programme |
| Alumni | Graduated and archived |

Only **Active** students appear in class rosters and attendance sheets.

## Subject enrolment

Admission puts a student in a programme; it does not by itself enrol them in
subjects. Enrolment is per subject, per semester, and each row has a status:

- **Active** — currently studying the subject
- **Completed** — finished, result published
- **Dropped** — withdrawn from the subject

A student appears on a teacher's roster only when both conditions hold: they
are in the section the class is taught to, **and** they are enrolled in that
subject. Section membership alone would include students who dropped it.

## Documents

Identity and academic documents (CNIC or B-Form, previous certificates) are
uploaded against the student record. A student may view and upload their own;
administrators may view any. Document files are stored in the database itself
rather than on disk.

## Changing programme, batch or section

These are administrative actions, not self-service. A student who believes
their programme, batch or section is wrong should contact the administration
office — the assistant can show what is currently recorded but cannot change it.

## Common questions

**Can I enrol myself in a subject?** No. Enrolment is administered centrally.

**Why does my portal show no subjects?** Either enrolment for the semester has
not been completed yet, or your academic status is not Active. The
administration office can confirm which.
