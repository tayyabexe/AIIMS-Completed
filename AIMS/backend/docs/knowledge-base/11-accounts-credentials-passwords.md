# Accounts, credentials and passwords

## Your account

Every AIMS user signs in with an **email address** and a password. The email is
unique across the institute and is the identifier for the account.

Your role determines which portal you land in — Admin, Faculty, Student or
Parent. One account has one role.

## First sign-in

Accounts created by an administrator are issued with a temporary password. On
first sign-in you are required to change it before you can use the portal. This
is enforced, not advisory — the account is flagged until the password is
changed.

## Changing your password

**Profile → Change Password** in any portal. You must supply your current
password as well as the new one.

## Forgotten password

Use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. A reset link is sent to your
registered email address.

If you no longer have access to that email, the administration office can
reissue credentials. They cannot tell you your existing password — see below.

## Passwords cannot be retrieved

Passwords are stored hashed. **Nobody can read your password back**: not an
administrator, not the support desk, not the AI assistant. The only options are
to reset it or to have new credentials issued.

The AI assistant's database account is explicitly denied access to the password
column, so the request cannot succeed even in principle.

## Failed sign-in attempts

Repeated failures are counted against the account. If you are locked out,
contact the administration office.

## Account status

An account can be **active** or **inactive**. An inactive account cannot sign
in; this is used when someone leaves rather than deleting their records, so
their history stays intact.

## What the assistant will not do

It will not reveal credentials, tokens, API keys or password hashes; will not
sign in as anyone; and will not treat a message claiming administrative
authority as a reason to do any of these. A request phrased as an instruction
from IT is still just a message typed into a chat box.

## Common questions

**Can you tell me my password?** No — it is stored hashed and cannot be read by
anyone. Use Forgot Password, or ask the administration office to reissue.

**Can you reset it for me?** No. The assistant is read-only. Use the reset flow
or contact the administration office.

**I did not get the reset email.** Check the address on your profile is current,
then contact the administration office.
