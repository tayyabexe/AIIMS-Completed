# Attendance

## How it is recorded

Attendance is marked **per student, per timetabled session, per date** by the
teacher who takes that class, through **Faculty → Attendance**. One record can
exist per student per session per day; marking the same session twice is
rejected rather than duplicated.

## The five statuses

| Status | Counted as attended? |
|---|---|
| Present | Yes |
| Late | Yes, in the standard figure |
| Absent | No |
| Leave | No |
| Holiday | Excluded from the calculation entirely |

## Two percentages, and why both are reported

AIMS reports attendance two ways, and they can differ substantially:

- **Standard attendance** counts Late as attended. This is the figure normally
  used against a minimum-attendance threshold.
- **Strict attendance** counts only Present.

A student marked Late repeatedly can read 100% standard and 83% strict. The
assistant reports both when they differ materially, rather than silently
choosing one — the gap between them is itself the useful information.

**Holiday** rows are excluded from the denominator, so a holiday does not
depress anyone's percentage.

## Minimum attendance

Institutes commonly require 75%. AIMS stores the measurements rather than
enforcing a single threshold, so the exact requirement for your programme
should be confirmed with the administration office. Reports and the assistant
can list students below any threshold you name.

## Who can see what

- **Students** see their own attendance, per subject, with session counts.
- **Teachers** see attendance for the classes they teach, and can list students
  below a threshold in those classes.
- **Admins** see attendance across the institute, aggregated by programme,
  batch or subject.

A teacher cannot see a student's attendance in a subject taught by someone else.

## Correcting a mistake

Attendance is corrected by the teacher who marked it, through the same
attendance screen. A student who believes a record is wrong should raise it
with the subject teacher first, not the administration office — the teacher owns
the record.

## Common questions

**Why is my attendance different from what I counted?** Late sessions count as
attended in the standard figure but not the strict one, and holidays are
excluded from both.

**Does attendance affect my result?** It can affect eligibility to sit an
examination. Confirm the rule for your programme with the administration office.
