# Things students ask to do that students do not do

Every entry here names the person who *does* do it, because knowing that is
usually what the question was actually for.

## How do I mark my own attendance?

You do not, and neither does any student. Attendance is marked **for** you by
the teacher who takes the class, per timetabled session, per date. AIMS has no
self check-in.

If a session is missing or marked wrongly, raise it with that **subject
teacher** — they marked it and they are the only person who can correct it.

## How do I mark attendance for a class?

That is a teacher's action, done from the Faculty portal, which student
accounts cannot open. If you believe an attendance record is wrong, the subject
teacher is who to speak to.

## Can I change or add my own marks?

No. Marks are entered and verified by the **subject teacher**, then published
by the **examination section**. There is no route by which a student edits a
mark.

## Can I see my marks before they are published?

No — and neither can your parent. Marks sit in Draft and Verified before
Published, and students see published marks only.

Ask your subject teacher whether marking is finished. "Not visible" almost
always means "not published yet".

## Can I reset another student's password? Or my own, as an admin would?

No. Use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen; a reset link goes to your
registered email.

If you cannot reach that email, the **administration office** issues new
credentials. Nobody can read your existing password back to you — it is stored
hashed, and that is true for every account including administrators'.

## Can I enrol myself in a subject, or change my section?

No. Enrolment, sections, batches and programmes are all managed by the
**administration office**.

## Can I pay a fee inside AIMS, or mark one as paid?

No. Pay through the channel printed on the voucher. The **accounts office**
verifies it afterwards, and it shows as **pending** until they do — which is
normal, not an error.

## Can I see another student's attendance, marks or fees?

No. Every screen is filtered to your own account, and the assistant is under
the same restriction. There is no phrasing of the question that changes this.

## Can I apply for a re-check through the portal?

No. Re-checks and appeals are an **examination section** process, handled as an
office procedure rather than a portal feature.

## Can I change the timetable, or request a different class time?

No. The timetable is built by the **administration office**.

## Can I upload my assignment?

Not into AIMS. There is no coursework submission feature — the Documents screen
is for identity and academic documents. Submit work however your teacher has
asked; the mark is what AIMS records.

## Can I post an announcement?

No. Announcements are posted by teachers to their classes and by administrators
more widely.

## Who do I contact, in one list

| Problem | Who |
|---|---|
| Attendance wrong or missing | Your subject teacher |
| Marks wrong, missing or unpublished | Your subject teacher, then the examination section |
| Fee, voucher or payment | The accounts office |
| Enrolment, section, programme | The administration office |
| Name, CNIC, date of birth | The administration office |
| Re-check or appeal | The examination section |
| Cannot sign in | IT support |
