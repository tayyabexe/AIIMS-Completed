# Assignments

## What an assignment is in AIMS

An assignment is an **assessment**, stored the same way an exam is, with its
type set to `Assignment`. It has a title, a subject, a due date and a total
mark.

That is worth knowing because it explains the behaviour people find surprising:
assignments appear alongside quizzes, midterms and finals wherever assessments
are listed, and they contribute to a subject percentage in exactly the same
way.

## Creating an assignment

**Faculty → Assignments**. A teacher creates one for a subject they teach,
setting the title, the due date and the total marks.

A teacher can only create assignments for their own subjects and sections.

## Is there submission tracking?

**No.** AIMS does not record per-student submissions. There is no upload box
for a student to submit work into, no submitted/not-submitted list, and no late
flag.

What exists is the **mark**. Once a teacher grades a piece of work and enters a
mark against the student for that assignment, there is a record; before that,
there is nothing recorded for that student.

So "has this student submitted?" is not a question AIMS can answer. "Has this
student been given a mark for it?" is.

Work is collected however the teacher and class have agreed — in person, by
email, or through whatever the department uses. AIMS records the outcome, not
the handover.

## Marking an assignment

Through the same marks flow as any other assessment: **Faculty → Marks**,
selecting the assignment. Marks are entered, verified, and then published by
the examination section.

An assignment mark is not visible to the student until it is published, the
same as any other mark.

## How an assignment affects a grade

Each assessment's percentage is obtained ÷ total × 100. Where a subject has
several assessments, the subject percentage is the **average of each
assessment's own percentage**.

That averaging is deliberate: it means a 10-mark assignment carries the same
weight as a 100-mark final in the subject percentage, rather than being
swamped by it. If a different weighting is used in your programme, confirm it
with the examination section.

## Where do students see their assignments?

Assignments appear with the rest of a subject's assessments under **My Courses**
(`/student/my-courses`), and any published mark appears under **Results**.

A student cannot see an assignment mark that has not been published yet.

## Common questions

**Can a student upload their work into AIMS?** No. There is no submission
upload. The Documents screen is for identity and academic documents, not
coursework.

**Can I see who has not submitted?** No — AIMS has no submission record. You
can see who has no mark entered yet, which is a different thing and may or may
not mean the same in your class.

**Can a student see the class average for an assignment?** No. Students see
their own marks only.

**Can a teacher change an assignment's due date after creating it?** Yes, from
the same Assignments screen, while it is theirs.
