# Dashboards

Every portal opens on a dashboard. Each figure on it is counted from live
records at the moment the page is read — none of them is estimated, cached
overnight, or produced by the AI.

## Student dashboard

`/student`. Shows your attendance summary, your next classes, any outstanding
fee balance, and recent notices.

- **Attendance** is the standard figure, which counts Late as attended. The
  strict figure, which counts only Present, is on the Attendance screen. If the
  two differ you have been marked Late at least once.
- **Fee balance** excludes payments still pending verification, so a payment
  you have made but the office has not yet verified does not show as owing.
- **Next classes** come from your section's timetable for today.

## Faculty dashboard

`/faculty`. Shows your teaching load, today's classes, and summaries across the
classes you teach.

- **Class performance** compares attendance and marks percentages for each
  subject-and-section you teach.
- **Grade distribution** counts published results only.
- Everything is limited to the classes on your timetable. There is no
  institute-wide figure here, because a teacher does not have institute-wide
  scope.

## Admin dashboard

`/admin`. Institute-wide KPIs: enrolment, attendance, fee collection, and
account health.

The dashboard carries a **read-at timestamp** and a refresh control. This is
deliberate — a dashboard left open on a desk goes stale silently, and a figure
with no timestamp cannot be trusted or corrected.

## Parent dashboard

`/parent`. A summary per child linked to your account: attendance, published
results, fee balance and upcoming classes.

You see what your child sees. Unpublished marks are not visible to you either.

## Why does a dashboard figure differ from a report?

Usually the date range. A dashboard shows a default period — commonly the
current semester or the current month — while a report shows the range you
chose.

Both read the same records, so a difference means the filters differ.

## Why is a figure zero?

Most often because the underlying records do not exist yet rather than because
something is broken:

- Zero attendance early in a term means no sessions have been marked yet
- Zero results means nothing has been published yet
- Zero enrolment means the semester's enrolment has not been run

## Common questions

**How often does the dashboard refresh?** When you load or refresh the page. It
is not live-updating.

**Can I change what is on my dashboard?** No, the layout is fixed per role.

**Is the dashboard generated by AI?** No. Every figure is a database count. The
AI features are separate — see *The AI Assistant and AI Analytics*.
