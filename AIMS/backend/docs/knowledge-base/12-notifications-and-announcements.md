# Notifications and announcements

Two different things, often confused.

## Notifications — addressed to you

A notification is generated by an event affecting **your** account: a fee
voucher issued, a result published, attendance falling below a threshold, or a
credential change.

Each carries a title, a message, a priority (normal, warning or success), and
usually a link to the screen that answers it — a notification about a voucher
opens that voucher.

Notifications are marked read once you open them. Find them under
**Notifications** in your portal.

## Announcements — addressed to a group

An announcement is a notice posted to a role or a group: a holiday, an
examination schedule, a registration deadline.

- **Admins** can post to any role.
- **Teachers** can post to their classes.
- **Students and parents** read announcements addressed to them.

You see announcements targeted at your own role, plus any addressed to
everyone. Announcements are not personal, so they are not marked read.

## Which one do I want?

| You want | Look at |
|---|---|
| Why your balance changed | Notifications |
| Whether results are out | Notifications, then Results |
| The exam timetable for the term | Announcements |
| A holiday or closure | Announcements |

## Through the AI assistant

Ask "any announcements for me" or "what notices are there". The assistant shows
announcements for your role, most recent first. It will not show you notices
addressed only to other roles.

## Not receiving something

Notifications are generated by events. If a result has been published and you
have no notification, check the Results screen directly — the record is the
authority, and the notification is a convenience.

Announcements depend on being addressed to your role. If you believe you are
missing one, the administration office can confirm who it was sent to.
