# The Faculty portal

Everything here is scoped to the classes on your timetable.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | `/faculty` | Teaching summary and pending tasks |
| My Classes | `/faculty/classes` | Every subject and section assigned to you |
| Attendance | `/faculty/attendance` | Mark and review class attendance |
| Marks | `/faculty/marks` | Enter, verify and publish exam marks |
| Assignments | `/faculty/assignments` | Assignment setup and submissions |
| Students | `/faculty/students` | Rosters for the classes you teach |
| Timetable | `/faculty/timetable` | Your weekly teaching schedule |
| Reports | `/faculty/reports` | Class performance and attendance reports |
| Announcements | `/faculty/announcements` | Post notices to your classes |
| Profile | `/faculty/profile` | Your staff profile and settings |

## Marking attendance

1. **Attendance**, choose the subject and section, and the date.
2. Mark each student Present, Absent, Late, Leave or Holiday.
3. Save.

One record exists per student per session per date. Re-saving corrects the
existing record rather than adding a second.

## Entering marks

1. **Marks**, choose the class and the exam.
2. Enter each student's obtained marks out of the exam total.
3. Save as **Draft** while working.
4. **Verify** when checked.
5. **Publish** to release to students.

Students see nothing until you publish. Drafts and verified marks are excluded
from class averages too, so a half-entered sheet does not distort your
statistics.

## Reports available to you

- Class performance: average percentage and pass rate per class
- Attendance summary: every student in a class, lowest attendance first
- At-risk students: low attendance or low CGPA, within your classes
- Your workload: sessions, subjects, sections and contact hours

## What you cannot reach

- Students you do not teach
- Fee and payroll data
- Institute-wide reports
- Another teacher's classes

If you need any of these, the administration office holds them.

## Using the AI assistant

Ask in plain English — "which of my students have attendance below 70%",
"how did my classes perform in the mid-term", "who is at risk in CS202".

The assistant is limited to your classes automatically. You never need to say
which students you mean for scoping, and you cannot widen it by asking
differently.

For questions that combine things — attendance against marks, say — it can query
the data directly, still limited to your own students.
