# Search, announcements and notifications

## Portal search

Every portal has a search box in its top bar. It searches **what your role can
already see**, and nothing else — it is not a way around any restriction.

- A **student** searches their own courses, timetable, results, fee vouchers
  and documents.
- A **teacher** searches the classes they teach and the students on those
  rosters.
- An **administrator** searches institute-wide records.

Selecting a result navigates to the screen that shows it.

## Why does search not find a student I know exists?

Because they are not in your scope. A teacher who does not teach that student
will not find them, by design — the search reflects the same rule the rest of
the portal applies.

## Announcements versus notifications

These are different things and are commonly confused.

An **announcement** is something a person wrote and addressed to an audience: a
teacher posting to their classes, an administrator posting institute-wide. It
has an author and a body.

A **notification** is generated by the system when something happens to your
records: a result was published, a fee is due, attendance dropped. Nobody wrote
it.

## Posting an announcement

Teachers post to their own classes from **Faculty → Announcements**.
Administrators post more widely from the Admin portal.

A teacher cannot post to a class they do not teach.

## Reading notifications

The bell in the top bar, and the **Notifications** screen in each portal. The
count is unread-only.

Marking one as read marks it read everywhere — the bell, the list and any other
screen showing it read from the same record, so there is no way for the count
to disagree with the list.

## Can I turn notifications off?

Notification preferences are on the **Settings** screen where the portal offers
them. Credential and security notifications — "your password was changed" — are
always delivered, to every role, because their purpose is to let you notice an
account takeover you did not perform.

## Common questions

**I did not get a notification about my result.** Check whether the result is
actually published; an unpublished result generates nothing.

**Can I reply to an announcement?** No. Announcements are one-way. Contact the
author through the usual channel.

**Can a student post an announcement?** No.

**Why can I not delete a notification?** Notifications are marked read rather
than deleted, so the record of what you were told stays intact.
