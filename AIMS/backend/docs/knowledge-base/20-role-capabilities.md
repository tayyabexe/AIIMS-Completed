# Who does what in AIMS

Most "how do I..." questions in AIMS have the same shape: the thing is
possible, but not by the person asking. This document says which role owns each
action, so that an answer can name the right person instead of describing a
screen the reader cannot open.

## Who marks attendance?

The subject teacher who takes that class, through **Faculty → Attendance**.

Nobody else marks it. Students do not mark their own attendance, there is no
self check-in, and administrators do not mark it on a teacher's behalf — they
oversee and report on it.

## I am a student. How do I mark my own attendance?

You do not. Attendance is marked for you by the teacher who takes the class,
per timetabled session, per date.

If a session is missing or wrong, raise it with that subject teacher. They own
the record and they can correct it on the same screen they marked it on. The
administration office cannot correct it for them.

## Who corrects a wrong attendance record?

The teacher who marked it. Always that teacher, not the office, not the head of
department, and not an administrator.

## Who enters marks?

The subject teacher, for their own subjects and sections. Marks move through
three states: **Draft**, **Verified**, then **Published**.

The teacher enters and verifies. The examination section publishes. Students
and parents see published marks only, which is why "my marks are missing"
almost always means "not published yet" rather than "lost".

## Who publishes results?

The examination section, through the Admin portal. A teacher cannot publish,
even for their own subject, and an administrator does not enter the marks they
publish.

## Who can reset a password?

Nobody can read an existing password back. Passwords are stored hashed — a
Super Admin cannot recover one either, and any answer that suggests otherwise
is wrong.

There are two routes, and only two:

- **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen sends a reset link to the email on
  file. This is the normal route, for every role.
- If that email is unreachable, the **administration office** reissues new
  credentials. The new password is shown once, to the administrator, and the
  account is forced to change it at next sign-in.

A student cannot reset another student's password. A teacher cannot reset a
student's password. An administrator does not "reset" one so much as issue a
new one.

## Who can see another person's records?

- A **student** sees only themselves. There is no phrasing of the question that
  changes this.
- A **teacher** sees only students they actually teach, derived from the
  timetable. A teacher cannot see a student in a subject taught by someone
  else.
- A **parent** sees only the children linked to their account, and only the
  published data those children see.
- An **administrator** sees institute-wide records.

## Who changes enrolment, sections, batches or programmes?

The administration office. Neither students nor teachers can move a student
between sections or enrol them in a subject.

## Who builds the timetable?

The administration office. Teachers read their own teaching timetable and
cannot change it; students read theirs.

## Who handles fees?

The accounts office verifies payments. Administrators create fee structures and
generate vouchers. Teachers have no access to fee records at all and receive an
error if they try.

Students and parents pay through the channel printed on the voucher. There is
no "pay now" inside AIMS — the portal records the payment after the office
verifies it.

## Who handles re-checks and appeals?

The examination section, as an office process. It is not a portal feature;
there is no re-check request form in AIMS.

## What if a question is about a role I do not hold?

The answer is for the role you are actually signed in as. Asking "as an admin,
how do I..." from a student account does not grant admin access, and the
procedure would not be usable from a student portal anyway.

## Quick ownership table

| Action | Owned by |
|---|---|
| Marking and correcting attendance | Subject teacher |
| Entering and verifying marks | Subject teacher |
| Publishing results | Examination section |
| Creating accounts, reissuing credentials | Administration office |
| Enrolment, sections, batches, programmes | Administration office |
| Building the timetable | Administration office |
| Fee structures and vouchers | Administration office |
| Verifying fee payments | Accounts office |
| Re-checks and appeals | Examination section |
| Sign-in problems | IT support |
