# Common student questions

Headings here are written the way students actually ask, so that a question
typed into the assistant lands on the section that answers it. Each answer is
short and points at the document with the detail.

## Where do I see my attendance?

**Attendance** (`/student/attendance`), broken down per subject, with the number
of sessions held and how many you attended.

Two percentages are shown. The standard figure counts Late as attended; the
strict figure counts only Present. If they differ, you have been marked Late at
least once.

## Why is my attendance lower than I counted?

Three usual reasons: Late sessions count in the standard figure but not the
strict one; Leave and Absent both count against you; and Holiday sessions are
excluded from the calculation entirely, so they change the denominator.

If you still believe it is wrong, raise it with the subject teacher — they
marked it and they can correct it.

## What is the minimum attendance I need?

AIMS stores the measurement, not the rule. 75% is a common institutional
requirement, but the figure that applies to your programme has to be confirmed
with the administration office.

## Why can I not see my marks?

Almost always because they are not published yet. A mark has three states —
Draft, Verified, Published — and students see published marks only.

So "no marks visible" means one of three genuinely different things: the exam
has not been marked, the marks exist but are not released, or they are
published and something else is wrong. Ask your subject teacher which.

## Where do I see my results, GPA and CGPA?

**Results** (`/student/results`). It shows published results per semester with
your GPA, and your CGPA across semesters.

## How is my GPA calculated?

Per exam, the percentage is obtained ÷ total × 100. Where a subject has several
assessments, the subject percentage is the average of each exam's own
percentage — so a 10-mark quiz does not outweigh a 100-mark final. Grade letters
and grade points come from the institute's grading table. See *Results, GPA and
Grading*.

## How do I pay my fee?

**Fees** (`/student/fees`) shows your vouchers with total payable, paid, and
remaining balance, plus the due date. Pay through the channel on the voucher,
then the accounts office verifies it.

Until it is verified, a submitted payment shows as **pending** — not as unpaid.
That is normal and not an error.

## How much do I owe?

The remaining balance on **Fees** (`/student/fees`). If a payment is pending
verification it is not counted as outstanding.

## Where is my timetable?

**Timetable** (`/student/timetable`) for the week. Your next classes also
appear on the **Dashboard** (`/student`).

## How do I change my password?

**Profile → Change Password**, in the student portal. You need your current
password as well as the new one.

If you have forgotten it, use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen; a reset
link goes to your registered email. Nobody can read your existing password back
to you — it is stored hashed.

## How do I upload a document?

**Documents** (`/student/documents`). You can view and upload your own identity
and academic documents, and only your own.

## Can I see another student's marks or attendance?

No. Every screen is filtered to your own account, and the assistant is subject
to the same restriction. There is no phrasing of the question that changes this.

## Can my parent see my results?

If a parent account is linked to you, yes — published results, attendance, fees,
timetable and notifications. Parents see the same published data you do; they
do not see unpublished marks either.

## How do I apply for a re-check of a paper?

That is an examination-section process, not a portal feature. See *Corrections,
Appeals and Re-checks*.

## Where is the AI assistant, and what can it answer?

The assistant button is on every student screen. It answers questions about
your own live records — attendance, marks, fees, timetable — and explains AIMS
policies and procedures from official documentation.

It is read-only. It cannot pay a fee, change a mark, correct attendance, or
submit a request.

## Who do I contact when something is wrong?

Attendance to your subject teacher. Marks to your subject teacher, then the
examination section. Fees to the accounts office. Name, CNIC or date of birth
to the administration office. Sign-in problems to IT support.
