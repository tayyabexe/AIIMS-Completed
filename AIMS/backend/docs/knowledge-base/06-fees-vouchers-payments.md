# Fees, vouchers and payments

Currency throughout AIMS is **PKR**.

## Fee structures

A fee structure defines what a semester of a programme costs. Vouchers are
generated from it, so a student's payable amount comes from their programme and
semester rather than being entered by hand each time.

## Vouchers

A voucher (also called a challan) is one bill issued to one student. It carries
a unique voucher number, an issue date, a due date, and three amounts:

- **Total payable** — what is owed
- **Amount paid** — what has been received
- **Remaining balance** — the difference

### Voucher statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unpaid | Nothing received |
| Partial | Some received, balance outstanding |
| Paid | Settled in full |
| Overdue | Past the due date and not settled |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn; no longer owed |

A voucher becomes **Overdue** once its due date has passed and it is neither
Paid nor Cancelled.

## Payments

Payments are recorded against a voucher. Accepted methods: **Cash**, **Bank
Transfer**, **Card**, **Mobile Wallet**, **Online**, **Cheque**, **Other**.

### Payments are verified

Every payment has its own status — **Pending**, **Verified** or **Rejected** —
with a record of who verified it and when.

This matters to students: a payment you have submitted but which has not yet
been verified is **not the same as not having paid**. AIMS tracks the two
separately, and the assistant reports them separately, so a recently submitted
payment awaiting verification is described as such rather than as an
outstanding balance.

## Who can see what

- **Students** see their own vouchers, balances, due dates and payment history.
- **Admins** see collection summaries by programme and semester, and the
  defaulter list with amounts and days overdue.
- **Teachers** do not have access to fee data at all.

## Late payment

Payments can be flagged as late. Whether a late fee applies, and how much, is an
institute policy rather than something the software imposes — confirm with the
accounts office.

## Common questions

**I paid but the portal still shows a balance.** Check whether the payment shows
as Pending. Verification is a manual step by the accounts office.

**How do I get a receipt?** Payments carry a receipt number once recorded.
Receipts are available in the student portal under Fees.

**Can the assistant pay a voucher or change a balance?** No. The assistant has
read-only access to the database and cannot alter any record.
