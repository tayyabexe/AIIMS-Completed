# Things teachers ask to do that teachers do not do

## Can I see a student I do not teach?

No. Your scope is derived from the timetable: you see a student when the
timetable puts you in front of their section for a subject they are enrolled
in, and not otherwise.

For a student outside your classes, the **administration office** holds the
institute-wide record.

## Can I publish results?

No. You **enter** and **verify** marks; the **examination section** publishes
them.

That split is why a student may tell you their mark is missing while you can
see it plainly: yours is Verified, theirs would need to be Published.

## Can I change a student's enrolment, section, batch or programme?

No — all four belong to the **administration office**. A student in the wrong
section on your roster is fixed there, not on your screen.

## Can I see or change fee records?

No. Teachers have no fee access at all and receive an error on those endpoints
by design. Fee questions go to the **accounts office**.

## Can I reset a student's password?

No. The student uses **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. If their
registered email is unreachable, the **administration office** reissues
credentials.

You cannot see their password either — nobody can, as passwords are hashed.

## Can I create an account for a new student?

No. Admission and account creation are done by the **administration office**,
which creates the login, the student record and the parent link together.

## Can I change the timetable?

No. The timetable is built by the **administration office**. You read your own
teaching schedule from **Faculty → Timetable**.

## Can I correct attendance a different teacher marked?

No, and this is deliberate: the teacher who marked a session owns it. A
correction to their record is theirs to make.

## Can I see who has submitted an assignment?

AIMS has no submission record. What exists is the **mark** — see *Assignments*.
"Who has no mark entered yet" is answerable; "who has not submitted" is not.

## Can I post an announcement to the whole institute?

No, only to your own classes. Institute-wide announcements come from the
**administration office**.

## Can I run an institute-wide report?

No. Your reports cover the classes on your timetable. Institute-wide reporting
is in the Admin portal.

## Can I mark attendance for a past date?

Yes, through the same attendance screen — attendance is recorded per session
per date, so a date can be selected. Marking the same session twice is rejected
rather than duplicated.

## Who do I contact, in one list

| Problem | Who |
|---|---|
| A student missing from my roster | The administration office (enrolment or section) |
| Results not published | The examination section |
| No classes assigned to me | The administration office (timetable) |
| A fee question from a student | The accounts office |
| A student cannot sign in | IT support, then the administration office |
| My own account or department detail | The administration office |
