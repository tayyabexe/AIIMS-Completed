# Corrections, appeals and re-checks

This document covers what to do when a record is wrong: a mark, an attendance
session, a fee, or a personal detail. AIMS records these things; it does not
adjudicate them, so every route below ends with a person.

## How do I apply for a re-check of a paper?

A paper re-check is an **examination-section process, not a portal feature**.
There is no re-check request form in the student portal, and the assistant
cannot submit one for you.

To request one, contact the examination section in writing. Bring or quote:

- your registration number,
- the subject and the exam (Quiz, Assignment, Mid-Term, Final, Practical, Viva),
- the semester,
- the mark as published in **Results** (`/student/results`).

The deadline after result publication, the fee, and whether a re-check is a
re-total or a full re-assessment are all set by the institute rather than by
AIMS. Confirm all three with the examination section before you pay anything.

## What happens in AIMS if a re-check succeeds

The subject teacher or the examination section edits the mark, and it is
re-published. Because a published mark is what students, parents and class
averages all read, the corrected figure replaces the old one everywhere at
once — your result, your GPA, and the class average recalculate from it.

An amended mark is an administrative correction and is recorded in the audit
log. Nothing is deleted.

## My marks look wrong. Who do I ask?

Ask in this order, and stop when it is resolved:

1. **The subject teacher.** They entered the mark and they can correct it.
2. **The examination section**, if the teacher cannot resolve it or the result
   is already published.
3. **The administration office**, if neither can.

Before raising it, check that the exam is actually published. "I sat the exam
but see nothing" is usually an unpublished mark rather than a lost one — see
*Examinations and Marks*.

## My attendance is wrong. Who do I ask?

**The teacher who took that class**, through their attendance screen. The
teacher owns the record; the administration office cannot correct it for them.

Check first that you are not reading the strict percentage: Late counts as
attended in the standard figure but not the strict one, and holidays are
excluded from both. A gap between the two figures is usually the explanation.

## My fee record is wrong. Who do I ask?

**The accounts office.** A payment you have submitted but which is not yet
verified shows as pending, not as unpaid — that is not an error, and it clears
when accounts verifies it.

Raise with accounts: a payment verified against the wrong voucher, a voucher
for a subject you are not enrolled in, or a balance that does not match your
receipts.

## My name, CNIC or date of birth is wrong

**The administration office.** Students and parents cannot edit identity fields
in the portal, deliberately — these are matched against admission records.

You *can* change your own password and, where the institute allows it, your
contact details, from **Profile** in your portal.

## Who can change what

| Record | Corrected by | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | The teacher who marked it | Faculty → Attendance |
| A mark, before publication | The entering teacher | Faculty → Marks |
| A mark, after publication | Teacher or examination section | Faculty → Marks, as a correction |
| Fee payment or voucher | Accounts office | Admin → Fees |
| Identity details | Administration office | Admin → Students |
| Your password | You | Profile → Change Password |

## What the assistant can and cannot do about a correction

It can tell you **what is currently recorded** and **who owns the record**. It
cannot change anything: it is read-only, has no write access to any table, and
cannot submit a request, file an appeal, or notify a teacher on your behalf.

Asking it to "fix my attendance" will get you the name of the person who can.
