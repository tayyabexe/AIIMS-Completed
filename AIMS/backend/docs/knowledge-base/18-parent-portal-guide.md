# The Parent portal

## Screens

| Screen | Route | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | `/parent` | Summary for each child you are linked to |
| Attendance | `/parent/attendance` | Your child's attendance per subject |
| Results | `/parent/results` | Published results, GPA and CGPA |
| Fees | `/parent/fees` | Vouchers, balance and payment history |
| Timetable | `/parent/timetable` | Your child's weekly class schedule |
| Notifications | `/parent/notifications` | Fee, result and attendance alerts |
| Profile | `/parent/profile` | Your details and password change |

## What you can see

The records of the children linked to your account, and nobody else's. The link
is made by the administration office when the parent account is created; it is
not something you can add yourself.

If you have more than one child at the institute, the portal shows each of them
and you choose between them.

## What you cannot see

- **Unpublished marks.** You see exactly what your child sees: published results
  only. A mark that has been entered, or even verified by a second member of
  staff, is not visible to either of you until it is published.
- **Another family's records**, under any circumstances.
- **Teacher-side screens.** Marking attendance, entering marks and posting
  announcements are staff functions.

## Reading your child's attendance

Per subject, with sessions held and sessions attended. Two percentages are
reported: the **standard** figure counts Late as attended, the **strict** figure
counts only Present. A gap between them means your child has been marked Late.

Holiday sessions are excluded from both, so a holiday does not depress the
percentage. See *Attendance*.

## Reading your child's results

**Results** (`/parent/results`) shows published results per semester, with GPA
and CGPA. If your child has sat an exam and nothing appears, the marks have not
been released yet — that is different from having no marks. See *Examinations
and Marks*.

## Fees

**Fees** (`/parent/fees`) shows vouchers with the total payable, what has been
paid, the remaining balance and the due date.

A payment that has been submitted but not yet verified by the accounts office
shows as **pending** rather than counting as unpaid. It clears when accounts
verifies it. See *Fees, Vouchers and Payments*.

## Notifications

Alerts about fees, results and attendance appear under **Notifications**
(`/parent/notifications`). See *Notifications and Announcements*.

## Your account

You sign in with your own email address and your own password — not your
child's. Change it from **Profile → Change Password**; use **Forgot Password**
on the sign-in screen if you cannot remember it.

Never sign in using your child's account to see their records. The parent
account exists so that you do not have to, and so that the institute can tell
the two apart.

## Using the AI assistant

The assistant explains how AIMS works: what the attendance percentages mean,
why a result is not visible yet, how vouchers and verification work, and which
screen to look at.

It does not read your child's records. For actual figures — attendance
percentages, marks, balances — open the relevant screen in the portal. The
assistant will tell you which one.

It is read-only in every sense: it cannot pay a fee, change a mark, correct
attendance, or contact a teacher for you.

## Who to contact

| Concern | Who |
|---|---|
| Attendance record | Your child's subject teacher |
| A mark or a result | The subject teacher, then the examination section |
| A re-check or appeal | The examination section |
| Fees and payments | The accounts office |
| Your details, or the parent-child link | The administration office |
| Cannot sign in | IT support |
